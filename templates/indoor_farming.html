Greenhouse & Vertical Farming Guide

Optimized strategies for controlled environments (Greenhouses, Hydroponics, and Indoor Systems).

🌡️ Climate Control

  • Temperature: Maintain 21°C–29°C for most veg. Use shade nets if >30°C.
  • Humidity: Keep at 60-70%. High humidity leads to mold; low leads to stress.
  • Ventilation: Open side vents daily. Use circulating fans to strengthen stems.

💡 Lighting & Energy

  • Grow Lights: Use full-spectrum LEDs for indoor vertical farms (12-16 hours/day).
  • Greenhouses: Clean roof plastic regularly to maximize natural sunlight.
  • Energy Saving: Use reflective mulch to bounce light back onto plants.

🦠 Common Indoor Diseases

Disease Cause Solution
Powdery Mildew High humidity, poor airflow Increase fan speed, reduce humidity, apply sulfur fungicide.
Root Rot Overwatering, stagnant water Check drainage, oxygenate hydroponic water, use hydrogen peroxide.
Whiteflies Warm, sheltered environment Use yellow sticky traps, introduce ladybugs (predators).
