Field & Open Air Farming Guide

Strategies for traditional farming, dealing with weather patterns, and soil management.

Soil Management

  • Crop Rotation: Never plant the same crop family twice in a row to prevent soil nutrient depletion.
  • Testing: Test soil pH annually. Add lime if too acidic (pH < 6.0).
  • Mulching: Cover soil with organic dry matter to retain moisture during drought.

Weather Defense

  • Heavy Rain: Dig trenches/furrows to direct excess water away from roots.
  • Drought: Water deeply in the evening to minimize evaporation.
  • Wind: Plant tree borders or taller crops (like Maize) as windbreaks for vegetables.

Seasonal Planting Guide (Kenya Region)

Season Months Recommended Action
Long Rains March - May Plant main crops (Maize, Beans) early. Watch for blight.
Short Rains Oct - Dec Plant fast-maturing crops (Beans, Veggies).
Dry Season Jan - Feb Prepare land. Solarize soil (kill pests with heat). Irrigate if possible.
