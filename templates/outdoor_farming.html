Soil & Defense

1
Crop Rotation
Never plant Solanaceae (Tomato/Potato) back-to-back. Rotate with Legumes to fix nitrogen.
2
Weather Proofing
Dig trenches/furrows to direct excess water. Plant maize windbreaks.
3
pH Balancing
Test soil annually. Add lime if pH < 6.0 to unlock nutrients.
