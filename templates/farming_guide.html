Common Indoor Risks

  • Root Rot (Pythium): Caused by low oxygen in water. Solution: Add air stones or H2O2.
  • Tip Burn: Calcium deficiency due to rapid growth. Solution: Increase airflow over leaves.
  • Algae Growth: Light hitting nutrient water. Solution: Cover reservoirs with opaque material.

Field Strategy

1. Crop Rotation
Prevent soil exhaustion. Follow Maize with Beans to fix nitrogen.
2. Trenching
Ensure drainage channels are clear before the Long Rains (March-May).
3. Solarization
In dry season (Jan-Feb), cover moist soil with clear plastic to kill pests/weeds with heat.
